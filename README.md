# IELTS

🧩 IELTS Writing Task 1 Template (Academic)

Goal: Describe, compare, and summarize visual data (graph, chart, table, process, or map).
Length: ~150–170 words

🧱 Structure Template

🔹 Paragraph 1 – Introduction (Paraphrase the question)

The [graph/chart/table/diagram] illustrates/shows/gives information about __________ over the period of __________ in __________.

Alternative phrases:

The given [chart/diagram] compares the proportion/number of __________.

The figure provides data on __________.

🔹 Paragraph 2 – Overview (Key trends, no numbers)

Overall, it can be seen that __________, whereas __________.
It is also noticeable that __________ showed the most significant change/was the highest/lowest among all.

Alternative openers:

In general, it is clear that…

A striking feature is that…

Overall, the most notable trend is that…

🔹 Paragraph 3 – Details (Main features + comparisons)

In [year/time/category], the figure for __________ stood at __________, while __________ was recorded at __________.
Between [year1] and [year2], __________ increased/decreased steadily, reaching __________.
By contrast, __________ experienced a slight rise/fall to __________.

Useful phrases:

“In comparison,” / “Similarly,” / “By contrast,”

“The proportion of X was approximately…”

“There was a gradual/rapid rise in…”

🔹 Paragraph 4 – More details (Remaining data or exceptions)

Meanwhile, __________ remained relatively stable at around __________.
In contrast, __________ fluctuated throughout the period.
Overall, while __________ increased, __________ saw a noticeable decline.

✅ Key tip:
Always have:

1️⃣ Introduction

2️⃣ Overview

3️⃣ Two detail paragraphs
Keep tense consistent (past, present, or future).

✍️ IELTS Writing Task 2 Template (Essay)

Goal: Present a clear argument with examples and logical structure.
Length: ~260–280 words

🧱 Structure Template

🔹 Paragraph 1 – Introduction
1️⃣ Paraphrase the question
2️⃣ Give your opinion/thesis statement

It is often argued that __________. While some people believe that __________, I firmly agree/disagree with the view that __________.
This essay will discuss both sides of the argument and explain why I believe that __________.

Alternative phrasing:

There has been much debate about whether __________.

Although some individuals think that __________, I personally believe that __________.

🔹 Paragraph 2 – Body 1 (Main idea 1)
1️⃣ Topic sentence (main argument)
2️⃣ Explanation
3️⃣ Example

One of the main reasons why I believe that __________ is because __________.
This is due to the fact that __________.
For instance, __________.

Useful connectors:

Firstly / To begin with / One major reason

As a result / Consequently / Therefore

🔹 Paragraph 3 – Body 2 (Main idea 2 or counterargument)
1️⃣ Topic sentence
2️⃣ Explanation
3️⃣ Example
4️⃣ (Optional) Refute opposite view

Another significant factor is that __________.
In addition, __________ plays a vital role in __________.
For example, __________.
Although some people argue that __________, I believe that __________.

Useful connectors:

On the other hand / However / Nevertheless

Furthermore / Moreover / Additionally

🔹 Paragraph 4 – Conclusion

In conclusion, it is clear that __________.
Therefore, I strongly believe that __________.
Overall, the advantages of __________ outweigh its disadvantages.

Alternative closing lines:

To sum up, both sides have valid arguments, but I am convinced that __________.

In summary, __________ is essential for __________ in the future.

💡 BONUS: Connector & Vocabulary Bank
Purpose	Phrases

Giving examples	For example, For instance, Such as, Namely
Result	Therefore, Consequently, As a result, Hence
